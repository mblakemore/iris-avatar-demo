# Iris — OpenAvatar demo

A talking head-and-shoulders avatar rendered live in the browser with WebGL 2 (3D Gaussian splatting, no libraries).

- Drag to turn her head; scroll or pinch to zoom.
- Press **Hear her speak**, or click her, to play a sample line with lip movement driven by the audio.

Files: `index.html` (the page), `openavatar-viewer.js` (the viewer module), `iris.bin` (the avatar, about 17 MB), `sample.mp3` (the sample line).
